Beelink mail

Last updated November 15, 2026

Beelink mail is a personal, single-user email assistant. It runs on its owner's home server and sorts the owner's own Gmail inbox: important mail stays in the inbox, and everything else is archived and listed in a once-a-day digest so nothing is missed.

Who can use it

Only its owner. Beelink mail is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not distributed. The software only accepts a connection to the one Gmail account it is configured for.

What it does with Gmail

  • Reads message headers and the beginning of each new message to decide whether it belongs in the inbox.
  • Archives, labels and marks messages as read in the owner's account.
  • Never sends, deletes or unsubscribes from email, and never shares email with anyone.

See the privacy policy for exactly what data is used and where it goes.
